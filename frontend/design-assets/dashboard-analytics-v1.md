Ilustración del dashboard
========================

Método: herramienta integrada imagegen, con fondo transparente.
Archivo: frontend/public/img/dashboard-analytics-v1.png.
Dimensiones: 1536 × 1024. Se conserva el canal alfa generado.
Uso: cabecera decorativa del dashboard. Representa análisis y organización
del almacén; sus gráficas son ilustrativas, no son indicadores de la aplicación.

Prompt final:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative transparent PNG hero illustration for a warehouse analytics dashboard. Create a polished premium 3D isometric still life centered on a cobalt-blue and white freestanding analytics tablet, tilted slightly backwards, with a clean symbolic bar chart and a circular chart (no words, letters or numbers). Behind and beside it, a compact white warehouse shelving unit with cobalt structural accents holding a few small kraft parcels, and a small handheld barcode scanner resting beside the tablet. Clear hierarchy: analytics tablet is the main subject; shelving is secondary. Restrained realistic matte materials, softly rounded edges, clean enterprise product design, warm cardboard and cool royal blue #2563EB, navy #1E3A8A, pale blue and white. Cohesive compact composition viewed isometrically, landscape roughly 3:2, fully visible with generous padding, legible at 360px wide. Genuinely transparent alpha background, no floor platform, no background panel, no wall, no colored haze or outer glow; only very subtle local contact shadows. No text, logos, watermark or people. Do not include a hand trolley, an open shipping box or a clipboard; this asset must be visibly distinct from a shipping-boxes-and-trolley illustration while belonging to the same blue warehouse visual family.
