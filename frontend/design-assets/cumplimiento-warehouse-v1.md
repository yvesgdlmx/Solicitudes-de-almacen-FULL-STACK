Recurso visual de cumplimiento de surtido
=======================================

Método: herramienta integrada imagegen; no se usó la API ni el CLI.
Archivo utilizado: frontend/public/img/cumplimiento-warehouse-v1.png.
PNG de 1536 × 1024 con canal alfa transparente, conservado sin modificaciones.
Se usa como ilustración decorativa de la cabecera y de los estados vacíos.
Los datos, porcentajes y barras de progreso de la pantalla proceden de la API.

Prompt final:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG illustration for the header of an existing blue and white warehouse operations dashboard. Create one elegant premium 3D isometric logistics still life: a compact group of three warm kraft cardboard shipping boxes with cobalt blue tape, one box open showing clean pale-blue packing material, a small blue warehouse hand trolley behind them, and a subtle small white clipboard with a blue checkmark leaning beside a box. No people. Rounded bevels, realistic matte paper, restrained studio lighting, soft natural contact shadows only near objects. Clean, mature enterprise product aesthetic, friendly but not toy-like. Main palette cardboard honey tan, royal/cobalt blue #2563EB, navy #1E3A8A and white. Composition one cohesive compact object cluster, landscape 3:2 approximately, fully visible with generous padding so nothing is cropped, lower eye-level isometric view. Isolated on genuinely transparent alpha background; absolutely no floor plane, backdrop, rectangle, gradient background, typography, letters, logos or watermark. The artwork must remain clear when rendered at around 340 by 220 CSS pixels and sit seamlessly on a pale blue UI background.
