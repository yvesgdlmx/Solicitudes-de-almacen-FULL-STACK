Ilustraciones de solicitudes
===========================

Método: herramienta integrada imagegen, con transparencia real conservada.
Las ilustraciones son decorativas y no contienen indicadores de la aplicación.

Mis solicitudes: frontend/public/img/mis-solicitudes-v1.png.
Todas las solicitudes: frontend/public/img/todas-solicitudes-v1.png.
Ambas imágenes tienen dimensiones de 1536 × 1024.

Prompt final de Mis solicitudes:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: genuinely transparent PNG hero illustration for a personal warehouse requisitions page. One premium polished 3D isometric still life: a blue and white document inbox tray holding a few clean white requisition sheets with abstract blue checklist lines, a cobalt pen leaning across the front, one modest closed kraft parcel beside the tray, and a small blue circular plus symbol as a physical object beside it. No words, letters, numbers or logos. Emphasize creating and organizing one's own requisition documents. Mature enterprise product aesthetic, matte realistic materials, softly rounded edges, same family as a blue warehouse operations app: royal blue #2563EB, navy #1E3A8A, white, warm kraft cardboard, restrained pale blue highlights. Compact cohesive composition, isometric front three-quarter view, landscape 3:2, generous clear margins with all objects fully visible. Legible at 340 CSS pixels. Actual transparent alpha background with no floor plane, no platform, no backdrop rectangle, no wall or colored haze; only soft localized contact shadows. No people, analytics tablets, warehouse shelves, hand trolleys, bar charts or watermarks.
